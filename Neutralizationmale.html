Lip Neutralization for Male

Achieve smooth, even lip color with Lip Neutralization—the perfect solution for male looking to correct dark or pigmented lips. This advanced procedure is designed to neutralize unwanted lip tones and bring out your natural, balanced lip color, providing a more polished and youthful appearance. Whether your lips have uneven pigmentation due to genetics, smoking, or other factors, Lip Neutralization creates a more uniform and refreshed look without the need for makeup.

Benefits of Lip Neutralization

  • Evens Out Lip Color: Lip Neutralization works to correct uneven pigmentation, ensuring your lips look natural and uniform in tone. This procedure is ideal for male who may have darker or discolored lips from genetics or other factors.
  • Prepares for Additional Enhancements: After neutralizing your lips, you're perfectly primed for further cosmetic treatments, such as lip tinting or blushing, for a fuller, more defined look.
  • Long-Lasting Results: Lip Neutralization is a semi-permanent solution that can last up to 1-3 years with proper aftercare, so you don’t have to worry about constant touch-ups or applying lip color every day.
  • Enhances Natural Features: This procedure enhances your lips without altering their natural shape, providing a subtle yet impactful improvement that complements your overall look.

Great for Male Who:

  • Have Uneven Lip Color: If your lips are naturally dark or discolored, Lip Neutralization provides an effective solution to even out your lip tone and restore a balanced, natural appearance.
  • Prefer Low-Maintenance Beauty Solutions: If you're looking for a way to enhance your lips without the need for constant upkeep or makeup application, this procedure is a great fit.
  • Want a Subtle Enhancement: Lip Neutralization gives you a more refined, polished look without the drastic changes often associated with cosmetic lip enhancements. It’s perfect for male who want natural-looking results.
  • Desire a More Youthful Look: By creating a smoother, more uniform lip color, Lip Neutralization can make your lips appear fresher and more youthful, enhancing your overall facial features.

The Lip Neutralization Process

Aftercare and Maintenance

The procedure begins with a consultation to determine your desired lip tone and assess your skin’s undertones. Once you're happy with the color choice, the lip artist will carefully apply pigment to neutralize darker or uneven areas. This treatment is designed to restore a natural, uniform lip color, offering subtle yet effective results. Numbing cream is used throughout the process to ensure maximum comfort.

Aftercare and Maintenance

To ensure optimal results, it's important to follow the aftercare instructions provided by your technician. Avoid direct sun exposure, excessive moisture, or irritating products on your lips during the healing period. With proper care, Lip Neutralization can last between 1 and 3 years, giving you a low-maintenance solution to beautiful, balanced lips.

What to expect

Your Journey With Us

1

Online Consultation

First, through WhatsApp, share a picture of your lips without any makeup in natural light. Our expert team will assess how much darkness needs correcting and suggest which type of procedure is most suitable for you.

2

Booking Your Slot

Once decided, book your dedicated time slot for your procedure. A booking deposit is mandatory to secure your appointment; this deposit will be deducted from your total procedure cost on the day of your visit.

3

In-Clinic Consultation

On your appointment day, before starting the procedure, a thorough in-person consultation will be provided to clear all your doubts and set proper expectations.

4

Topical Anesthesia

The procedure begins with the application of a topical anesthesia cream. We let this sit for 20 minutes over your lips to ensure a completely comfortable and numbing effect.

5

Bespoke Mapping

After numbing, your natural lip line is mapped with precision, fully customized to your specific likes, preferences, and facial symmetry.

6

Outline Approval & Procedure

Once you are 100% happy with the outline shape, we will go ahead with layering corrective tones to balance the darker pigmentation before an even, natural lip colour is built up.

7

The Reveal

In the next 1 hour, the magic happens. You will see your lips looking the absolute best with your stunning new look.

8

Aftercare Instructions

After your first session, our team will brief you on strictly guided aftercare instructions that need to be followed for optimal healed results and pigment retention.

9

Follow-up Touchup

Wait for one month for complete skin healing. A follow-up touchup session can be taken anytime between 30 - 90 days. Every skin heals differently naturally. Depending on the healing, definition and darkness can be enhanced in this second session.
