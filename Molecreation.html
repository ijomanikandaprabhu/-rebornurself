Mole Creation for Female

Enhance your beauty with a timeless, elegant touch! Mole creation is a subtle cosmetic procedure that adds charm and character to your face by placing beauty marks in strategic, flattering locations. Whether you're inspired by classic Hollywood glamour or looking to balance facial features, mole creation can be the perfect finishing touch to elevate your natural allure.



Why Choose Mole Creation?

  • Highlight Your Best Features: Moles placed near the lips, eyes, or cheeks can subtly draw attention to your facial features, enhancing your overall expression and elegance.
  • Personalized Placement: Each mole is artistically positioned based on your unique facial symmetry and aesthetic goals, ensuring a natural, flattering result that complements your beauty.
  • Classic & Timeless Appeal:From beauty icons of the past to modern-day influencers, a well-placed mole has always been a symbol of sophistication and allure.
  • Safe & Quick Procedure: The process is minimally invasive and typically takes less than an hour, with little to no downtime. You'll walk out feeling transformed—with a new beauty mark to match your style.
  • Low Maintenance: Unlike makeup, your new beauty spot won’t smudge or fade throughout the day. It’s a long-lasting enhancement that stays effortlessly perfect.


Who is Mole Creation For?

Mole creation is an ideal option for Female seeking a subtle, sophisticated beauty enhancement. It’s perfect for those who appreciate the artistry of vintage glamour or wish to bring balance and focus to certain facial features. Whether you’re going for a bold signature look or a discreet touch of elegance, this procedure adapts to your personal style.

  • Beauty Enthusiasts: Add a unique signature to your face that sets you apart and adds personality to your look.
  • Busy Professionals: Achieve a polished, put-together appearance with minimal effort—your beauty spot stays flawless all day, every day.
  • Lovers of Vintage Glamour: Channel the charm of iconic stars like Marilyn Monroe or Cindy Crawford with your own statement beauty mark.
  • Those Seeking Facial Balance: Strategic placement of a mole can enhance symmetry and highlight the areas of your face you love most.
  • Sensitive Skin Types: Since the procedure doesn’t involve makeup, it's a great solution for those who are allergic or sensitive to cosmetics.
What to expect

Your Journey With Us

1

Online Consultation

First, through WhatsApp, share a picture of the area without any makeup in natural light. Our expert team will study your features and suggest the placement and size that will suit you best.

2

Booking Your Slot

Once decided, book your dedicated time slot for your procedure. A booking deposit is mandatory to secure your appointment; this deposit will be deducted from your total procedure cost on the day of your visit.

3

In-Clinic Consultation

On your appointment day, before starting the procedure, a thorough in-person consultation will be provided to clear all your doubts and set proper expectations.

4

Topical Anesthesia

The procedure begins with the application of a topical anesthesia cream. We let this sit for 20 minutes over the area to ensure a completely comfortable and numbing effect.

5

Bespoke Mapping

After numbing, the exact placement is marked according to your facial structure, fully customized to your specific likes, preferences, and facial symmetry.

6

Outline Approval & Procedure

Once you are 100% happy with the marked placement, we will go ahead with creating a natural-looking beauty spot in the tone and size you have chosen.

7

The Reveal

In the next 1 hour, the magic happens. You will see your new beauty spot sitting naturally, exactly where you wanted it.

8

Aftercare Instructions

After your first session, our team will brief you on strictly guided aftercare instructions that need to be followed for optimal healed results and pigment retention.

9

Follow-up Touchup

Wait for one month for complete skin healing. A follow-up touchup session can be taken anytime between 30 - 90 days. Every skin heals differently naturally. Depending on the healing, definition and darkness can be enhanced in this second session.
