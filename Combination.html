Combination Brows for Female

Achieve the ultimate brow perfection with Combination Brows! This innovative technique blends the precision of microblading's natural hair strokes with the soft, shaded effect of Ombre Powder Brows. The result is a multidimensional, flawless brow look that’s both natural and polished. Combination Brows are perfect for Female who want to add depth, definition, and symmetry to their brows while maintaining a soft, feminine touch.

Why Choose Combination Brows?

  • Depth and Definition: By merging two techniques, Combination Brows create a realistic, textured appearance with added dimension. Hair strokes mimic natural brow hairs, while soft shading enhances the fullness and shape.
  • Natural Yet Polished Finish: This method strikes the perfect balance between subtle enhancement and noticeable beauty. The brows look natural, but with a polished edge that accentuates your features.
  • Versatility for All Skin Types: Whether you have oily, dry, or combination skin, this technique works seamlessly to provide long-lasting results.
  • Ideal for Sparse or Uneven Brows: Combination Brows can fill in gaps, correct asymmetry, and create the illusion of fuller, well-balanced brows.
  • Long-Lasting Results: Enjoy up to 12–18 months of beautiful brows with minimal upkeep, making this a time-saving beauty solution for busy Female.

Perfect for Female Who:

  • Desire a natural enhancement with added dimension and depth.
  • Want to simplify their daily makeup routine and enjoy hassle-free beauty.
  • Have sparse, patchy, or uneven brows and want a solution that looks soft yet defined.
  • Appreciate a tailored approach that’s designed to suit their unique face shape, skin tone, and style.

The Combination Brows Process

Your journey to flawless brows begins with a personalized consultation, where we discuss your goals and assess your natural brow shape. Our brow artist maps out the perfect shape to enhance your facial features and creates a plan tailored specifically to your needs.

During the procedure, microblading is used to create fine, hair-like strokes that mimic natural brow hairs, followed by gentle shading to add depth and fill in any gaps. A numbing cream ensures your comfort throughout the process, and the result is a pair of brows that look effortlessly beautiful.

Aftercare and Maintenance

To ensure the best results, proper aftercare is essential. Avoid getting your brows wet, applying makeup, or exposing them to direct sunlight for the first week. A touch-up session is typically scheduled 4–6 weeks after the initial procedure to perfect the results and ensure long-lasting beauty.

Combination Brows give you the confidence to wake up every day with beautifully defined, natural-looking brows. It’s the ultimate solution for enhancing your natural beauty and simplifying your life.

What to expect

Your Journey With Us

1

Online Consultation

First, through WhatsApp, share a picture of your brows without any makeup in natural light. Our expert team will analyze your brows and suggest which type of procedure is most suitable for you.

2

Booking Your Slot

Once decided, book your dedicated time slot for your procedure. A booking deposit is mandatory to secure your appointment; this deposit will be deducted from your total procedure cost on the day of your visit.

3

In-Clinic Consultation

On your appointment day, before starting the procedure, a thorough in-person consultation will be provided to clear all your doubts and set proper expectations.

4

Topical Anesthesia

The procedure begins with the application of a topical anesthesia cream. We let this sit for 20 minutes over your brows to ensure a completely comfortable and numbing effect.

5

Bespoke Mapping

After numbing, meticulous hand-mapping is done according to your unique bone structure, fully customized to your specific likes, preferences, and facial symmetry.

6

Outline Approval & Procedure

Once you are 100% happy with the outline shape, we will go ahead with combining hand-drawn hair strokes at the front with soft shading through the body and tail.

7

The Reveal

In the next 1 hour, the magic happens. You will see your brows looking the absolute best with your stunning new look.

8

Aftercare Instructions

After your first session, our team will brief you on strictly guided aftercare instructions that need to be followed for optimal healed results and pigment retention.

9

Follow-up Touchup

Wait for one month for complete skin healing. A follow-up touchup session can be taken anytime between 30 - 90 days. Every skin heals differently naturally. Depending on the healing, definition and darkness can be enhanced in this second session.
