Ombre Powder Brows for male

Redefine your brows with subtle sophistication using our Ombre Powder Brows technique. Designed specifically for those who prefer a soft, polished look, this semi-permanent procedure enhances the natural appearance of your brows with a gradient effect—lighter at the start and gradually darker toward the ends. The result is a clean, defined brow that adds structure and symmetry to your face without appearing overly done.

Why Choose Ombre Powder Brows?

  • Tailored for Oily or Combination Skin: Ombre Powder Brows are perfect for male with oilier skin, as the technique offers a more durable and long-lasting finish compared to other methods.
  • Low-Maintenance Results: Enjoy effortlessly groomed brows every day without the need for daily touch-ups or excessive maintenance.
  • Natural Yet Defined Look: The customizable intensity of Ombre Powder Brows ensures your brows look sharp and well-defined while still maintaining a natural appearance that complements your features.
  • Subtle Enhancement: This technique avoids overly dramatic results, making it ideal for male who want a subtle improvement that enhances their overall look.

Perfect for Male Who:

  • Want to correct sparse or uneven brows while maintaining a masculine and natural aesthetic.
  • Prefer a polished, well-groomed appearance without the hassle of daily upkeep.
  • Desire a long-lasting solution for enhancing their brows that doesn’t look like makeup.
  • Appreciate a clean, symmetrical look that frames their eyes and defines their facial structure.

The Ombre Powder Brows Process

The process begins with a consultation to determine the ideal shape and intensity that suits your features and personal style. Our expert technicians carefully map your brows, considering your facial proportions and desired outcome. During the procedure, a gentle shading technique is used to create a gradient effect, ensuring a soft and natural finish. A numbing cream is applied to ensure your comfort throughout the session.

Once the procedure is complete, you’ll receive aftercare instructions to maintain your results. With proper care, Ombre Powder Brows can last anywhere from 1 to 3 years, making it a convenient and effective solution for male who value a well-groomed look.

What to expect

Your Journey With Us

1

Online Consultation

First, through WhatsApp, share a picture of your brows without any makeup in natural light. Our expert team will analyze your brows and suggest which type of procedure is most suitable for you.

2

Booking Your Slot

Once decided, book your dedicated time slot for your procedure. A booking deposit is mandatory to secure your appointment; this deposit will be deducted from your total procedure cost on the day of your visit.

3

In-Clinic Consultation

On your appointment day, before starting the procedure, a thorough in-person consultation will be provided to clear all your doubts and set proper expectations.

4

Topical Anesthesia

The procedure begins with the application of a topical anesthesia cream. We let this sit for 20 minutes over your brows to ensure a completely comfortable and numbing effect.

5

Bespoke Mapping

After numbing, meticulous hand-mapping is done according to your unique bone structure, fully customized to your specific likes, preferences, and facial symmetry.

6

Outline Approval & Procedure

Once you are 100% happy with the outline shape, we will go ahead with building a soft, misty shade that stays light at the front and deepens gradually towards the tail.

7

The Reveal

In the next 1 hour, the magic happens. You will see your brows looking the absolute best with your stunning new look.

8

Aftercare Instructions

After your first session, our team will brief you on strictly guided aftercare instructions that need to be followed for optimal healed results and pigment retention.

9

Follow-up Touchup

Wait for one month for complete skin healing. A follow-up touchup session can be taken anytime between 30 - 90 days. Every skin heals differently naturally. Depending on the healing, definition and darkness can be enhanced in this second session.
