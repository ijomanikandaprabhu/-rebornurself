Good to know

How Booking Works

1. Send us a photo first

Before you book, message us on WhatsApp with a photo of your bare brows or lips taken in natural light, with no makeup on. We use it to tell you which procedure will actually suit you, rather than guessing on the day.

2. Reserve your slot

Once we have agreed on the right procedure, pick a time that works for you. A booking deposit is required to hold the slot, and it is deducted from your total procedure cost on the day of your visit.

3. Set aside enough time

On the day we start with an in-person consultation, then a topical numbing cream that sits for around 20 minutes before any work begins. Plan for a relaxed appointment rather than a rushed one.

Nothing is permanent until you approve it

Your brow or lip shape is mapped by hand and shown to you first. We only begin the actual procedure once you are completely happy with the outline.

Aftercare matters

Healed results depend heavily on aftercare. Before you leave, we brief you on exactly what to do and what to avoid while your skin settles and the pigment retains.

Your touch-up session

Skin needs about a month to heal fully. A follow-up touch-up can be taken any time between 30 and 90 days, where definition and depth can be adjusted to suit how your skin healed.
