Finding the Studio

Where we are

Rebornurself is at A 5, NGO Nagar Main Rd, Alapakkam, New Perungalathur, Chennai, Tamil Nadu 600063. Use the map above for directions, and message us if you need help finding the entrance.

When we are open

The studio is open from 10:00 AM to 07:00 PM. Procedures are done by appointment, so please book your slot in advance rather than walking in.

The quickest way to reach us

WhatsApp is easiest, especially since your consultation starts with a photo of your bare brows or lips in natural light. You can also call +91 80901 11911 or email hello@rebornurself.com.

What we offer

Microblading, ombre powder brows, combination brows, lip neutralization, lip blushing and mole creation — each available for both women and men.

Not sure what you need?

That is normal, and it is exactly what the first consultation is for. Send us a photo and we will recommend the procedure that suits your features, your skin and the look you are after.

Before your appointment

Come with your brows or lips free of makeup so we can assess your natural shape and tone. Allow enough time on the day — numbing alone takes around 20 minutes before any work starts.
