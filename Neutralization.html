Lip Neutralization for Female

Say goodbye to uneven, dark, or pigmented lips and welcome a flawless, natural pout with Lip Neutralization! This semi-permanent cosmetic procedure is designed to correct discoloration, bringing out your lips' true, even color. Whether you're looking to balance out dark spots or simply enhance your natural lip shade, Lip Neutralization creates a smooth, uniform tone that enhances the beauty of your lips.

Benefits of Lip Neutralization

  • Evens Out Lip Color: Lip Neutralization is the perfect solution for lips that are uneven in tone, especially if you have darker areas or pigmentation caused by genetics, sun exposure, or smoking. The treatment creates a beautiful, uniform lip color that complements your natural skin tone.
  • Prepares Lips for Additional Enhancements: If you’re considering further lip treatments like lip blush or tint, Lip Neutralization serves as the perfect foundation by evening out your lips and creating a smooth canvas.
  • Long-Lasting Results: Unlike regular lip makeup, Lip Neutralization offers a long-lasting solution, meaning you can enjoy vibrant, naturally beautiful lips without the hassle of daily touch-ups.

Benefits of Lip Neutralization

  • Want to correct dark, uneven, or pigmented lips for a more uniform and natural appearance.
  • Prefer a low-maintenance approach to lip enhancement that doesn’t require daily makeup application.
  • Are looking for a long-lasting way to bring out the natural beauty of their lips.
  • Desire a subtle enhancement that complements their overall aesthetic and facial features.

The Lip Neutralization Process

The process begins with a consultation where we assess your natural lip color and discuss the desired outcome. We’ll choose a pigment that complements your skin tone and neutralizes any discoloration. The procedure involves applying this specially formulated pigment to the lips, blending it to create an even, beautiful result. A numbing cream is used to ensure comfort throughout the session.

After the treatment, your lips may be slightly swollen or tender, but this is temporary and normal. Results will fully settle in after a few weeks, and your lips will have a more uniform, natural look.

Aftercare and Maintenance

To ensure the best results, it’s important to follow aftercare instructions, which may include avoiding water exposure, makeup, or excessive sun exposure for a short period after the procedure. A follow-up touch-up may be scheduled after 4–6 weeks to perfect the color and ensure a long-lasting, flawless result.

Lip Neutralization is the perfect solution for anyone who wants smooth, evenly colored lips with minimal upkeep. It’s an easy, effective way to enhance your natural beauty and feel confident every day!

What to expect

Your Journey With Us

1

Online Consultation

First, through WhatsApp, share a picture of your brows without any makeup in natural light. Our expert team will analyze your brows and suggest which type of procedure is most suitable for you.

2

Booking Your Slot

Once decided, book your dedicated time slot for your procedure. A booking deposit is mandatory to secure your appointment; this deposit will be deducted from your total procedure cost on the day of your visit.

3

In-Clinic Consultation

On your appointment day, before starting the procedure, a thorough in-person consultation will be provided to clear all your doubts and set proper expectations.

4

Topical Anesthesia

The procedure begins with the application of a topical anesthesia cream. We let this sit for 20 minutes over your brows to ensure a completely comfortable and numbing effect.

5

Bespoke Mapping

After numbing, meticulous hand-mapping is done according to your unique bone structure, fully customized to your specific likes, preferences, and facial symmetry.

6

Outline Approval & Procedure

Once you are 100% happy with the outline shape, we will go ahead with creating hyper-realistic strokes or soft shading, depending on the chosen procedure.

7

The Reveal

In the next 1 hour, the magic happens. You will see your brows looking the absolute best with your stunning new look.

8

Aftercare Instructions

After your first session, our team will brief you on strictly guided aftercare instructions that need to be followed for optimal healed results and pigment retention.

9

Follow-up Touchup

Wait for one month for complete skin healing. A follow-up touchup session can be taken anytime between 30 - 90 days. Every skin heals differently naturally. Depending on the healing, definition and darkness can be enhanced in this second session.
